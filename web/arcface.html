Face Models

Two kinds of face model. A face-detection model finds faces in each frame, alongside your object model. An ArcFace embedding model then recognises who each face belongs to among your enrolled people. The embedding models are Apache-2.0 licensed (commercial use permitted).

Face Detection

An extra detector that looks for faces only. It runs at the same time as your object model, never replaces it. Turn it on and choose which installed model it uses under Settings.

Step 2 · Recognise who

Face Recognition

Each embedding model turns an aligned face crop into a 512-number faceprint that is matched against your enrolled people. Enrolments are compatible between models that share the same embedding space. Turn recognition on under Detection → Faces.
