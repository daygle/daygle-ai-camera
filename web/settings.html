Administration

Settings

Manage camera, recording, storage, notifications, and login settings.

⚡

Live Performance

Tune refresh rates and detection frequency to balance responsiveness with resource usage.

The live object overlay may appear slightly behind the video feed. This is normal on CPU hardware - AI inference takes time, and there is no further optimisation possible beyond the extrapolation already applied.

Advanced Motion Tuning

Low-level motion-detector internals - the defaults suit most cameras. Changing Motion Frame Width or Height resets every camera's background model.

🎬

Recording Clips

Control how event recordings are captured. Per-rule Record toggles are on the Zones page; Continuous Recording is per camera on the Cameras page.

🧹

Retention

Automatically clean up old recordings and events to manage disk usage.

📁

Storage

Configure where Daygle stores data on disk. Changes take effect after saving.

Set at install time and rarely changed. Paths are restricted to the application data directory and take effect after saving.

🔔

Push Notifications

Get instant alerts on your Android device via the ntfy app. Per-rule push toggles are on the Zones page. Install the ntfy app on your Android device and subscribe to your topic to receive alerts.

📡

Camera Offline Notifications

Get notified when a camera goes offline or recovers. Works with both email and push notification channels.

✉️

Email Delivery

Configure your SMTP mail server to send alert emails. Per-rule email toggles are on the Zones page.
